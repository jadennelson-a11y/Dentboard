What stays on your device

Your decks, slide images, notes, flashcard progress and settings are saved in your own browser on your own device. We do not have copies of them, and they do not sync between devices. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

What is sent to our AI service

When you generate questions, an outline, teach-back prompts, or ask a question, the relevant slide text and your question are sent through our server to an AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the result. Our server does not store this content. When you ask a question the AI may also search the web. We use your IP address only to limit how often the service can be used, and we do not keep it.

Other services

The "Latest research" list contacts PubMed (NCBI) directly from your browser. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

What not to upload

Please do not upload patient information or anything that identifies a patient. Only upload materials you have the right to use.

Your choices

You can delete any deck inside the app, and clear everything by clearing site data in your browser. Questions or requests: [contact email].
